Detailed Description

The ONEBEAD coarse-grained mapping of an all-atom structure.

Every amino acid is one bead, every nucleotide is two or three beads (sugar, base and, when present, phosphate), every monosaccharide is one bead and every monoatomic ion is one bead. A bead is the centre of mass of its atoms.

The mapping is built from an all-atom PDB (the template), read chain by chain and, within a chain, residue by residue. The template must contain exactly the atoms of the list passed to the constructor, in the same order. Atom indices in this class are positions in that list.
