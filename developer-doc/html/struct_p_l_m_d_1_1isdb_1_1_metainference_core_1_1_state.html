Detailed Description

The state that the Monte Carlo and the averages change at every evaluation.

Every evaluation of a step starts from the state at the beginning of the step, and update() keeps the state of the last evaluation before it: evaluations that are not followed by update() (replica-exchange trials, numerical derivatives, the further force calculations of a step with shells) change nothing. The sigma mean histories, which can be long, are not in it: the evaluation computes their maxima as if the new value were added, and update() adds it.
