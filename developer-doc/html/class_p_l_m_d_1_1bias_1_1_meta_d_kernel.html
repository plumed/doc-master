Detailed Description

Evaluation of the (possibly stretched) Gaussian hills of METAD as a function of its arguments.

Gaussians are truncated at dp2cutoff. With INTERVAL (one dimensional only) the CV is clamped to [lower,upper] and the force outside the interval is zero.

value of hill at a point, given the differences dp of the point from the center of the hill (multiplied by invsigma for diagonal hills, plain differences for multivariate hills); the derivatives are subtracted to der.

Outside the cutoff it returns 0, leaves der unchanged and sets inside=false. Same arithmetic as evaluateWithDerivatives, INTERVAL is not taken into account.
