Detailed Description

Exact-restart checkpoint of a PLUMED run, configured by the CHECKPOINT action.

The checkpoint for step S captures the state at the start of S (after the update of step S-1 and before anything of S is computed) together with the sizes of all the output files after a flush at that moment. On restart the files are truncated to those sizes and the MD engine presents step S again, which is replayed in full. No action needs to know that a step is repeated.

The checkpoint is written when the MD engine sends the checkpointing signal (cmd "doCheckPoint") or, with STRIDE, every STRIDE steps. It is a separate file, one per replica (usual suffix), written atomically; the previous one is kept with a _prev suffix.
