Detailed Description

template<typename real>
struct PLMD::TrajectoryFrame< real >

A frame of a trajectory, as read by TrajectoryParser.

Lengths are in nm and times in ps (the readers convert from the units of the file); masses and charges are in the units of the file. The caller sets the inputs and provides the buffers, sized for TrajectoryParser::nOfAtoms() atoms (known after readHeader()).
