Detailed Description

The calculation of a single segment of ALPHARMSD_SCALAR, ANTIBETARMSD_SCALAR and PARABETARMSD_SCALAR.

These functions are used both by SecondaryStructureCount and by its GPU version in the openaccPTM plugin, so that the two give the same result. A segment is given by the first atoms of its two halves of 15 atoms (strands): for a helix they are consecutive.

Is the segment made of the strands starting at atoms s1 and s2 computed?

x are the coordinates of the atoms, pbc is null with NOPBC. It is not if the distance between the central CA atoms of the strands is larger than cutoff (if cutoff>0), as step(STRANDS_CUTOFF-x) in the shortcut, or, if radius is not null, if that distance minus the radii of the strands (see strandRadius, radius[s] is the one of the strand that starts at atom s) is larger than skipDistance: then the switching function of the segment is zero (see SecondaryStructureCount::setSkipDistance)

The positions of the atoms of the segment made of the strands starting at atoms s1 and s2, as in SecondaryStructureBase::performTask (pbc is null with NOPBC).

With alignStrands (the sheets) the strands are made whole separately, then the second is moved to the image where its central CA atom is closest to that of the first; otherwise (the helices, where s2=s1+15) the segment is made whole as a single chain of bonded atoms.

The switching function of the lowest distance of a segment from the reference structures (as LOWEST and LESS_THAN in the shortcuts).

It returns s(r) and sets g=ds/dr. If g is not zero, der[best*segmentDerivatives ...] contains the derivatives of r with respect to the positions of the atoms of the segment and the virial; with several structures the distances are first computed without derivatives, and the derivatives are computed only for the lowest one, and only if g is not zero.
