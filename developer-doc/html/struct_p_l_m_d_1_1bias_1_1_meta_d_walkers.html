Detailed Description

The multiple walkers of METAD and PBMETAD: they share their hills through the hills files (WALKERS_N), each walker reading the files of the others every WALKERS_RSTRIDE steps, or through MPI (WALKERS_MPI), the walkers being the replicas, which all add the hills of all of them and write them in the same files.
